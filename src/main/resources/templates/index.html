Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Vidya School Management platform.

What is Vidya and how does it benefit schools?

Vidya is an all-in-one School Management System and FinTech engine. It eliminates administrative overhead by digitizing admissions, attendance, report cards, and recurring fee collections through automated e-NACH and payment gateways.

How does recurring fee collection with e-NACH work?

Parents register an e-NACH mandate once via Net Banking or Debit Card. On the scheduled fee due date, Vidya automatically triggers bank debits, updates the ledger, and sends instant receipts via WhatsApp and Email.

Is tenant data securely isolated?

Yes. Vidya enforces dynamic datasource separation. Each educational institution operates within its designated tenant context, preventing any unauthorized cross-organization data access.

Can Vidya assist parents who need education loan financing?

Yes. Vidya features an integrated loan recommendation engine that assesses payer profiles and offers customized, low-interest or zero-cost EMI plans from partner financial institutions.
